Reflections on College Life

Lessons Learned

My university journey, culminating in my commencement on February 14, 2026, has been defined by resilience and adaptability. Transitioning from Physical Therapy to Information Systems proved to me that possessing a growth mindset is far more valuable than a fixed knowledge base. To me, college served as a comprehensive crash course on what to expect in the professional realm. It was only upon entering the workforce that I understood just how vast the field of Information Systems is. This realization has driven home the need for a deeper understanding in this industry; learning never truly stops.

Career Goals

My professional objective is to specialize in Data Science and system analytics, leveraging tools like Python and SQL to transform raw data into actionable insights. However, my recent experience as an Application Development Intern at Land Registration Systems, Inc. expanded my horizons, teaching me the joy of building tangible software solutions and navigating the software development lifecycle. Furthermore, developing my thesis project, MetroMetrics, allowed me to build machine learning-driven predictive models. Moving forward, I aim to cultivate expertise across both fields, positioning myself as a versatile developer who can derive insights from data and build the robust platforms that house them.

Commitment Statement

I am committed to a career of lifelong learning, particularly within the rapidly evolving fields of Artificial Intelligence and predictive analytics areas I deeply explored when contributing to a research paper on real-time commuter crowding. Beyond technical proficiency, I pledge to uphold the highest ethical standards regarding data privacy and integrity. I will strive to use my skills to create efficient, transparent solutions that solve real-world problems and contribute positively to the community and the industry at large.
